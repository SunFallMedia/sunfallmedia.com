Meet Jeremy Nagy

Founder & Editor, SunFall Media

Before founding SunFall Media, Jeremy spent ten years working in Kentucky's coal industry and more than fifteen years in geotechnical engineering. Those jobs gave him a close view of the communities, industries, and challenges that shape life across Kentucky and the Ohio River Valley. They also taught him to check the details, ask questions, and consider who is affected by a decision.

About Jeremy Nagy

A Kentucky native, Jeremy has spent most of his life living and working throughout the Commonwealth.

He began his career in Kentucky's coal industry, spending nearly a decade working in and around mining operations. Those years gave him firsthand experience with the people, culture, and challenges of Appalachian and coalfield communities.

After studying Environmental Science at Murray State University, Jeremy moved into geotechnical engineering, where he has worked for more than fifteen years.

Along the way, he kept returning to stories with unanswered questions: government decisions that affected local communities, missing-person cases, overlooked pieces of regional history, and mysteries that never seemed fully resolved. His reporting today covers government accountability, true crime, regional history, and unexplained mysteries across Kentucky, Indiana, and Ohio.

His approach is straightforward: gather the facts, check what can be checked, add context, and give readers room to reach their own conclusions.

Why I Started SunFall Media

Over the years, I found myself paying attention to stories that never seemed fully answered. Some involved a public decision that affected a community. Others were cold cases, missing people, or pieces of local history that had been forgotten. The questions mattered, even when the answers were hard to find.

I started SunFall Media to take a closer look. It's not here to tell people what to think or push a particular viewpoint. I want to gather the facts, ask better questions, and share what I can verify so readers can make up their own minds.

That's the standard I try to bring to every story: careful research, useful context, and a willingness to follow the evidence.

Our Mission

Stories rooted in truth and community

SunFall Media exists to tell the stories that communities need to know. We investigate cases others overlook, expose decisions made in silence, and explore the mysteries that linger in our collective memory.

Through rigorous reporting, intimate storytelling, and a commitment to accountability, we build a newsroom that reflects the complexity, depth, and humanity of the Ohio River Valley.

Alongside our core journalism, we've created platforms for creative voices—artists, writers, and musicians shaping the cultural conversation. SunFall Media isn't just a newsroom. It's a home for stories that matter.
